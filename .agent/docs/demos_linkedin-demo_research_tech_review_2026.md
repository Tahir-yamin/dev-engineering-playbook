## 🤖 1. The Rise of Agentic AI: Beyond the Prompt
Traditional Generative AI was reactive. **Agentic AI** is proactive. In early 2026, we've seen models move from "predicting text" to "managing outcomes."

### Key Technical Breakthroughs:
- **Contextual Planning**: Agents now possess long-horizon planning capabilities, allowing them to decompose a "goal" (e.g., "Reduce cloud spend by 15%") into 50+ localized actions across different APIs.
- **Self-Correction (FIO Protocol)**: Modern agents no longer stall at tool failures. They independently research workarounds, effectively implementing the "Figure It Out" protocol in real-time.
- **Neuromorphic Edge Deployment**: The shift to local neuromorphic chips has reduced latency for agentic decision-making by 85%, enabling "always-on" background operatives.
